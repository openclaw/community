# Safety and Incident Reference

Use this guide when a situation needs quick action. Protect people first, preserve useful context, and ask for help when the right response is unclear.

## Scams, Impersonation, and Suspicious Links

- Remove clearly harmful content.
- Do not debate or amplify the scam.
- Warn nearby users when there is a meaningful risk.
- Escalate to the moderators or Admin.

Crypto promotions, trading schemes, prediction markets, impersonation, and suspicious links deserve particular care because they can damage users and community trust.

## Doxxing or Personal Data

- Remove the content immediately.
- Preserve the relevant context for the moderation record.
- Support the affected person.
- Escalate to the Admin right away.

## Harassment, Threats, or Serious Disruption

- Protect the target and stop the immediate disruption.
- Use the approved moderation tools and record what happened.
- Escalate serious, repeated, or clearly malicious behavior.

## Spam, Raids, and Bots

- Remove spam and contain the disruption.
- Alert the moderators or Bot Managers when tooling or configuration may be involved.
- Preserve examples if the incident may need follow-up.

## Voice Incidents

- Disconnect or restrict disruptive users when needed.
- Use `/report-vc` whenever possible.
- Escalate repeated or serious incidents.

## Self-Promotion

Redirect useful project promotion to `#showcase`. Remove or escalate promotion that is spammy, unsafe, unrelated to the channel, or part of a broader abuse pattern.

## When Unsure

Pause before taking a strong action and ask another moderator, a lead, or the Admin. Immediate action is appropriate when someone is at risk; uncertainty is not a reason to ignore a serious safety issue.
