## Area map (quick)

Community Staff is not split into separate teams. These are areas of responsibility that staff work on together, with leads coordinating each area.

- **Admin / Community Lead**: final decisions, escalations, staffing, policy, and foundation/community coordination.
- **Moderation**: general rule enforcement across Discord text and voice spaces.
- **Help**: support channels, OpenClaw help, triage, and Clawtributor claim review.
- **Reddit**: r/OpenClaw and r/Clawdbot operations.
- **Events**: Weekly Claw and community events.
- **Bot Managers**: elevated permission group for bot/configuration access, not a separate area.
- **GitHub Moderators**: elevated permission group for GitHub moderation, not a separate area.

## Access + setup

- Confirm you have the correct staff role(s) and any needed elevated permissions.
- Read through the shared staff channel and any area-specific channels you can access.
- Talk to your lead about expectations, coverage needs, and any questions you have.

## First week checklist

- Shadow a lead or senior mod for a few cases.
- Handle at least one low-severity case with a lead available.
- Review the Incident Playbook (self-promo, crypto, doxxing, VC incidents).
- Practice `/warn`, `/mute`, `/ban`, and `/caselist`.
- Ask your lead what coverage windows are most needed.

## Guardrails

- When in doubt, mute for 1 hour with the reason "pending moderation" and ask for help.
- If you are unsure, ping online moderators in #moderators or the relevant staff channel.
- If you’re doing a voice moderation, use `/report-vc` whenever possible.
- Do not use native Discord moderation tools.

## Communication + activity

- Stay reasonably active (chat presence, VC presence, support answers, mod discussions).
- Read staff update announcements and react once read so leads can confirm visibility.
- Post inactivity/LOA notices in the appropriate staff channel and ping your lead.
- If Hermit sends you an activity warning, respond within two days so leads/Admin can discuss your availability.
- LOA format:
  - Dates: 12th June 2024 - 1st July 2024
  - Reason: Optional (DM a lead/Admin if you prefer)
