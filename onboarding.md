# Community Team Onboarding

Welcome to the Community Team. Our job is to help people use and contribute to OpenClaw while keeping the community safe and approachable.

## Read First

- [Community Team Guide](README.md)
- [Moderation Reference](moderation.md)
- [Safety and Incident Reference](incident-playbook.md)

## Getting Started

- Confirm that you have the access and permissions needed for your area.
- Read the relevant staff channels and ask your lead about current priorities.
- Learn how to use Krill and the approved moderation tools.
- Send setup, usage, and troubleshooting questions to **[Get support](https://community.openclaw.ai/)**, the official OpenClaw support pathway; keep Discord for social conversation and collaboration.
- Know the separate routes for development bugs, private security reports, and serious incidents in the [guide](README.md#support-and-discussion) and [incident reference](incident-playbook.md).

## First Week

- Shadow an experienced team member.
- Help with at least one straightforward support or community task.
- Review the common safety cases in the incident reference.
- Ask questions when a situation is unclear.

Stay involved and communicate when you will be away for a while so the team can plan coverage. There is no need to turn this into a formal reporting process.
