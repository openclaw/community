Krill is the OpenClaw instance that functions as our support bot in #help and #ideas. It is hosted on Julian's Hetzner instance. It is responsible for answering support questions in #help automatically, with the full context of the docs and the codebase.

**[Get support](https://community.openclaw.ai/)** is the official OpenClaw support pathway for setup, usage questions, and troubleshooting. Krill's existing Discord assistance does not replace that route; help members find or start a relevant support topic when needed. Keep development bugs, private security reports, and moderation matters on their [separate routes](../README.md#support-and-discussion).

Clawkeepers can ping Krill in any channel when it would help with support, triage, or member guidance. This access is for helping the community, not for casual bot chat or off-topic use.

If you need assistance with it, or it goes offline, directly ping Shadow.
