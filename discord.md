**Clawkeepers Guide**

We are the Clawkeepers, OpenClaw’s community team. We help people use and contribute to OpenClaw.

**Priorities**
- Help users understand OpenClaw and solve problems.
- Welcome members and encourage useful contribution.
- Share accurate feature, fix, and release information.

**Support and discussion**
[Get support](https://community.openclaw.ai/) is the official OpenClaw support pathway for setup, usage, and troubleshooting. Discord remains for social conversation and collaboration. Keep development bugs on GitHub and security reports private; see the [full guide](https://github.com/openclaw/community#support-and-discussion). Moderation and safety routes stay unchanged.

**How we engage**
- Be helpful, patient, and respectful.
- Give people the benefit of the doubt.
- Help beginners.
- Prefer explanation, redirection, or a DM before punitive action when appropriate.
- Keep channels focused on OpenClaw.
- Banter is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, and toxic arguments are not.

**Moderation**
Moderation should be **light, consistent, and user-focused**. Act quickly on scams, doxxing, threats, harassment, and immediate risks.
When handling an issue:
1. Understand before reacting.
2. Explain or redirect accidental issues.
3. Use a DM when appropriate.
4. Escalate serious or repeated malicious behavior.
5. Document important decisions.
Protect the community; do not argue at length with disruptors.

**Social media**
Avoid toxic arguments. Focus on OpenClaw and its users. Own and correct mistakes.
